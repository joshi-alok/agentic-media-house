Fixture Robotics has begun shipping its FX-4 warehouse arm to a second plant run by Brindle Logistics, at Tarnmoor.[1](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-1) The first site, at Ashvale, now runs 40 of the arms, and a third site is planned.[2](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-23)[3](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-26) The company also reported a $450 million funding round, led by Corvane Partners, in a regulatory filing.[4](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-17)[5](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-18)

## Key takeaways

- Tarnmoor is the second Brindle Logistics plant to receive FX-4 arms.[1](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-1)
- The datasheet lists a 12 kg rated payload and a 1.4 m maximum reach.[6](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-8)[7](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-9)
- Brindle Logistics measured an 18% cut in cycle time at Ashvale.[8](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-20)
- The filing dates the first sale of the $450 million round to 29 September 2026.[9](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-16)[4](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-17)

## What shipped

Fixture Robotics announced on 1 October 2026 that deliveries of the FX-4 warehouse arm to Brindle Logistics have started at Tarnmoor, the customer's second plant.[1](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-1)[10](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-2) The company says the arm is built for tote picking and trailer unloading.[1](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-1) It also says the arm mounts on existing conveyor frames without a new safety cage.[11](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-3) Fixture Robotics lists the arm from $38,000, with delivery within 12 weeks of an order.[12](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-4)

The datasheet lists a rated payload of 12 kg at full reach and a maximum reach of 1.4 m from the base.[6](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-8)[7](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-9) Peak tool speed is 2.3 m/s, and repeatability is 0.05 mm.[13](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-10)[14](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-11) The same document gives a throughput of up to 1,200 picks an hour.[15](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-12) The arm itself weighs 38 kg and draws 650 W in typical use.[16](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-13)[17](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-14) It lists an ingress protection rating of IP54, which the company describes as suitable for dusty warehouse floors.[18](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-15)

At Ashvale, the first Brindle Logistics plant, 40 FX-4 arms are now running.[2](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-23) Tarnmoor is due to receive 60 arms by 30 November 2026.[19](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-5) Brindle Logistics plans to use the arms at Tarnmoor for inbound trailer unloading.[20](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-25)

## What the customer reports

At Ashvale, Brindle Logistics measured an 18% cut in cycle time on its tote-picking cells.[8](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-20) It compared the four weeks before and after the arms went live.[8](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-20) The installation at Ashvale went live on 18 August 2026, according to Brindle Logistics.[21](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-24) "We went from pilot to production in nine weeks," said Dana Whitcombe, chief operating officer of Brindle Logistics.[22](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-22)

Across the same weeks, Ashvale averaged 940 picks an hour per arm.[23](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-21) **Analysis.** The measured rate sits below the datasheet maximum, which is normal for a figure quoted as an upper limit.[23](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-21)[15](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-12) At a demo day on 24 September 2026, Fixture Robotics showed an FX-4 arm unloading mixed cartons from a trailer.[24](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-27)[25](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-28) The demonstration took place in the company's three-bay test hall.[25](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-28)

Brindle Logistics plans to add a third site in 2027.[3](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-26) **Analysis.** The customer's results and the company's schedule together describe a rollout that is still at an early stage.[23](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-21)[3](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-26)

## What the filing shows

The round, led by Corvane Partners, totals $450 million, according to a regulatory filing.[4](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-17)[5](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-18) The filing records the first sale on 29 September 2026.[9](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-16) It lists 7 investors in the round.[26](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-19)

Fixture Robotics said it will use the funds to expand FX-4 production.[27](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-7) It expects to build 500 FX-4 arms in 2027.[28](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-6) **Analysis.** The stated use of the funds matches the production plan that the company describes.[27](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-7)[28](/news/fixture-robotics-ships-fx-4-to-second-plant/receipts/#c-6)
